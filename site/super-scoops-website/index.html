Super Scoops Website

I led UX and visual design to give Super Scoops, a beloved ice-cream shop in Treasure Island, Florida, a fun, low-maintenance web presence. I ran rapid discovery, then produced a playful one-page layout. Bold pink headlines and a cartoon ice-cream-cone mascot sit over collaged backgrounds of halftone dots, stripes, and bright swirls of color, all grounded by a warm cream base. The homepage moves through sections like Grab a pint to-go, Everyday is sundae, Take home a shirt, and Stop by for a scoop, with a Today's scoop photo row. The updatable menu lists ice cream, sundaes, Italian ice, soft serve, and special scoops with prices in clean columns. Instagram integration keeps the site current, and hours, address, and social links close out each page. I made sure the design was responsive, and the mockups show it on a tablet and a laptop. The site created a professional presence without upkeep overhead and supported a busy, growing shop.
