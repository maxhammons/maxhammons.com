VIBES

I partnered with the VIBES team on editorial direction and design for the VIBES report. I conducted interviews and gathered program data, then combined photography and infographics into spreads organized by section: Volunteering, Investment, Belonging, Equality, and Social Responsibility. Each section has its own color. Hand-drawn marker lettering, arrows, and sparkles turn figures like 73% of employees volunteering in 2023 and $302,063.66 in total employee giving into headlines. Program pages cover CoachThis!, MindAlign, and WellnessPalooza, and first-person journal entries run alongside the stats. Social Responsibility spreads show the office's 800 solar panels, composting and xeriscaping, and on-site produce used at the BoujieMana restaurant. I also managed print and launch. Employees felt energized and gained clear visibility into impact initiatives.
