A little about me
I’m a creative leader focused on building brand systems and UX that scales. My journey has taken me from the precision of agency work to the freedom of freelance, crafting a portfolio that’s as innovative as it is diverse.
I care about leaving the world a bit better than when I found it. I’ve teamed up with non-profits and mission-driven orgs that feed the hungry, shelter pets, and mentor students. Watching my work spark real-world change keeps me going.
When I’m off the clock you’ll catch me chasing soccer balls, soaking up mountain sunshine, or experimenting in the kitchen (follow the chaos at @cooking.with.max). Let’s grab coffee, swap ideas, and cook up the next big thing together.
