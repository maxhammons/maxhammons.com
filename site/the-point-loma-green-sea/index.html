Point Loma Green Sea

This is the graphic identity for the Point Loma Nazarene University Green Sea, the official student section of PLNU. The identity includes a sub-identity: The Green Sea Hooligans. The Hooligans are the leaders of the Green Sea and therefore needed a special logo and (of course) their own unique shirts. The main mark is a circular badge with a white curling wave at its center, POINT LOMA and GREEN SEA set in bold yellow capitals around it, and 1902 split on either side. The Hooligans badge keeps the same round structure but closes it in a solid white ring and swaps the wave for a sea lion. The shared format ties the two together, while the sea lion sets the leaders apart. Both logos are shown over green-tinted photos of students cheering in the stands, and each one is printed large on the back of its own green t-shirt.
