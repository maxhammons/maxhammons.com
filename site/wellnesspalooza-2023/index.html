WellnessPalooza 2023

I created an 80s retro-wave campaign system for TCWGlobal's Wellness Palooza, a wellness month that ran all August long. The primary logo sets a yellow brush-script wordmark over pink palm trees inside a glowing neon triangle, with alternate lockups in other colors and palm-tree treatments. The palette runs hot pink, electric blue, teal, and sunset orange against dark grids and gradient skies. Along with reusable templates, I designed event posters for Hike Club, Lunch Crunch, Flash Dance Friday, the Blood Drive, the Dental Clinic, and more. The same look carried into a promotional banner, a Weekly Wingo bingo card, a weekly fitness planner, and Deskercise and event calendars. It also showed up on an embroidered cap, a pennant, and badge stickers. I collaborated with planners to thread the theme into playlists and wardrobe touches. Participation hit record highs as cohesive visuals carried through the event for a memorable employee experience.
