BoujieMana

I directed a luxurious, over-the-top brand for the BoujieMana restaurant and a system for its adjacent La Maison Event Space. I designed a full menu suite set in deep green, with lowercase serif headings, ornamental corner flourishes, line-art spot illustrations, and the looping b monogram. A kids' coloring menu turns that line art into a dog, burgers, fries, and pizza to color in. I produced promotional signage and ads, including brunch and lunch and dinner posters that pair bold lowercase type with food photography. At the entrance, I applied a nature-inspired frosted glass treatment of monstera leaves. For La Maison, I created a custom icon set and detailed floor layouts for twelve seating configurations, from theater and classroom to banquet and cocktail hour. The cohesive system elevated the dining experience, clarified event wayfinding, and delivered a memorable, luxe vibe.
