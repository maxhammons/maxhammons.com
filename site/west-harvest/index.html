West Harvest

I directed branding, environmental graphics, and wayfinding for West Harvest, a high‑volume café on a Fortune‑50 tech campus in Menlo Park. The work moved from mood boards and two alternate concepts, Grateful Garden and Urban Market, through rounds of wordmark studies to a sage green logotype with a carrot mark. I created a distinct identity and mural series built from illustrated produce patterns in sage, coral, and cream, hung as framed art beside a dimensional wall sign. I designed digital menu boards and a signage system that runs from painted floor arrows like Have a Seat and Dish Return to exterior blade signs, plus branded shirts and caps. I prepared 200+ production‑ready files for fabrication and installation, including wall elevations with paint callouts, and managed on‑site reviews and placement approvals. The café became a stand‑out space with smooth peak‑time flows and a memorable atmosphere.
