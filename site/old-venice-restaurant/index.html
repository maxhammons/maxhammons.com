Old Venice Restaurant

I developed a typographic identity and menu and signage standards for Old Venice Restaurant that complement the décor rather than overpower it. I explored classical letterforms and monochrome treatments. The result pairs tall condensed capitals in white on charcoal bars, a red tag for the restaurant name, and a refined serif for menu copy, with the tagline Godere Insieme, enjoy together, running through the system. I carried it across logo lockups, a catering business card, menu sheets with red prices under Antipasti, Entrata, Pizza, and Speciali, a to-go bag printed I Tuoi Avanzi, and a website menu page shown on desktop and phone. Black-and-white photography of Venice and the dining room sets the mood. I collaborated with the venue team to harmonize with the florals and finishes. The elegant mark elevates the space while letting the setting shine.
