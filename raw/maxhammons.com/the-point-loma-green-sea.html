Point Loma Green Sea

This is the graphic identity for the Point Loma Nazarene University Green Sea. The Green Sea is the official student section of PLNU. The identity includes a sub-identity; The Green Sea Hooligans. The Hoolignas are the leaders of the Green Sea and therefore needed a special logo and (of course) their own unique shirts.
