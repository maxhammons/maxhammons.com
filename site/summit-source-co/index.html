Summit Source Co.

I owned the logo, palette, and typography standards for Summit Source Co. A competitive scan identified a modern lane for differentiation, and the identity was built to claim it. I started with hand-drawn sketches of mountain, anchor, and zigzag ideas, then refined faceted, dotted, outlined, badge, and monogram studies before landing on a double-peak mountain cut into angular facets. The palette pairs Sunset Glow orange with Midnight Navy and black, each specified in CMYK and RGB values for print and screen. The mountain icon locks up with a heavy italic wordmark in navy, orange, and black versions. From there I produced the guidelines and vehicle graphics, along with letterhead, business cards, and a branded navy mug. The polished system created a professional handshake across trucks, invoices, and web, improved differentiation, and increased credibility in B2B sales.
