Ambition Angels

I defined the brand platform for Ambition Angels: logo, color, type, and voice and tone. The wordmark pairs clean lettering with a red arrow mark and a circular icon, and it flexes into six lockups, from stacked to condensed to horizontal. The palette of Ambition Red, Potential Yellow, Aspiration Green, Possibility Blue, and Blue Sky sits beside the Epilogue type family. A loose, hand‑drawn doodle pattern and bold, color‑blocked slides carry the identity into presentations, a conference stage screen, an app interface, and a phone case. I delivered self‑serve Canva and Slides templates, plain‑language brand guidelines, and volunteer onboarding sessions so non‑designers could produce on‑brand materials. The identity generated rapid recognition and shortened turnarounds with less back‑and‑forth. It also simplified outreach to parents, schools, and partners and strengthened fundraising confidence.
