Niles Bistro

I designed and specified interior brand elements for Niles Bistro, a corporate bistro. I aligned the wall graphics and signage to the corporate brand, integrated them with the interior design, and oversaw production and installation with facilities. The concept treats the space as a circuit board, reflecting the company’s technology-focused, interconnected culture. Across the floor plan, a circuit-board wayfinding graphic links the food stations, with code-style labels like PIZZA.HTML. On the walls, colorful graffiti murals form the base layer, overlaid with dimensional white stripes that read as circuit traces. Glitched, distorted Bistro typography and meme-based imagery print onto backlit panels meant to look like digital screens, with binary code accents below. I specified each wall in dimensioned elevation drawings keyed to a location plan. The environment elevates daily dining and reflects company culture.
